# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this reference accurate — free tiers move fast.

## What we accept

- **Corrections** to a limit, price, or license that has changed (link the provider's own docs).
- **New providers** with a genuine free tier or free-forever open weights.
- **Dead-link fixes.**

## Rules that keep this useful

1. **Source every claim.** Link the provider's official pricing/docs page, not a blog.
2. **Stay qualitative where the provider hides the number.** If a provider has moved its limits behind a login (Google, Mistral), say "unpublished" — do not guess a figure.
3. **Check the weights license, not just the code badge**, for anything self-hosted.
4. Keep both `README.md` and `data/providers.json` in sync when you edit a row.

## How

1. Fork, branch, edit.
2. Open a PR describing what changed and linking your source.

That's it. Small, sourced, honest PRs get merged fast.
